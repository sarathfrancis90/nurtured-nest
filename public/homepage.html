Belly Binding (Postpartum)

Think of belly binding as a warm, supportive hug for your core after birth. This gentle postpartum practice helps support your muscles, improve alignment, and bring comfort as your body recovers. It is a simple yet powerful way to feel more held, supported, and put-together in early postpartum days.

Why Hire a Doula?

Birth is a life-changing experience, and having the right support can make all the difference.

A doula provides continuous emotional, physical, and informational support throughout your journey— helping you feel confident, informed, and cared for every step of the way.

  • Continuous care before, during, and after birth.
  • Calm, compassionate guidance to help you feel grounded and supported.
  • Breathing techniques, positioning, comfort measures, and hands-on support.
  • Advocacy and informed decision-making so you can choose confidently.
  • Partner support so they feel included, confident, and involved.
  • Personalized care tailored to your birth preferences.

You deserve to feel supported—whether you choose unmedicated birth, epidural, or cesarean.

FAQs

Questions you may have

What does a doula do?

A doula provides continuous emotional, physical, and informational support during pregnancy, labour, and postpartum. I help you feel informed, reassured, and supported in your choices while offering comfort techniques, encouragement, and guidance throughout your journey.

When should you hire a doula?

You can hire a doula at any stage of pregnancy—even early on or later in the third trimester. The earlier we connect, the more time we have to build trust and prepare together. It's never too late to reach out.

Do you replace my doctor or midwife?

No. I work alongside your medical team as a non-medical support person to help you feel emotionally and physically supported throughout your care.

What is the difference between a doula and a midwife?

A midwife provides medical care and monitors your and your baby's wellbeing. A doula focuses on emotional support, comfort techniques, and advocacy so you feel supported alongside your medical team.

Do you help me medically?

No. Doulas do not provide medical care, diagnosis, or procedures. I offer comfort measures, education, and emotional support.

Can my partner still be involved if I have a doula?

Absolutely. I support both you and your partner by helping them feel confident and involved while giving them space to rest and participate naturally.

What kind of support do you provide during labour?

During labour, I offer continuous support such as breathing techniques, positioning suggestions, massage, reassurance, and emotional grounding through each stage.

Do you offer postpartum support?

Yes. I provide postpartum support to help with emotional well-being, newborn care guidance, feeding support, and settling into your new routine.

How much does a doula cost?

My services vary by support level. Please contact me for package options that best fit your family.

I'm feeling anxious about birth—can a doula help?

Yes. I provide reassurance, education, and emotional support to help you feel prepared, calm, and confident as birth approaches.
